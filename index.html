How it works

Simple enough to use when words are difficult.

You don't need to create a perfect story. Say what you feel, discover someone who understands, and decide what happens next.

01

Write

Share a feeling, thought, experience, or moment in your own words.

02

Be understood

Other people can respond with “I Understand” instead of a generic like.

03

Connect

If a meaningful connection feels right, unlock it through the rewarded-ad system.

04

Talk

A verified connection opens private text chat for the active connection period.

Product experience

Designed to feel like a complete social platform.

Feed, discovery, profiles, notifications, messages, settings, and safety are part of the planned product architecture.

◉

Your feelings have a place to live.

Create posts, choose a mood or topic, discover experiences from other people, and build a personal profile around what you choose to share.

  • Personal profile
  • Feelings history
  • Discover and search
  • Notifications
◇

Human connection

Connection access is controlled through a verified rewarded-ad unlock and server-controlled expiry.

◌

Private conversations

No voice chat and no video chat. V1 private conversations are text-first and connection-based.

Connection model

Intentional connections, not unlimited access.

A rewarded advertisement is completed and verified before the trusted backend grants a 12-hour connection.
