Connect safely

Listen My Feelings uses a protected connection system.

The final connection flow requires a verified rewarded advertisement and trusted backend authorization.

Once authorized, connection access is limited to 12 hours and is controlled by server-side data rather than Local Storage.

This browser will not pretend that a connection is active before the trusted backend confirms it.

Safety & reporting

You can report harmful content using the Report button or block a person through the three-dot menu.

Never share your password, OTP, bank details, payment information or private identification with strangers.

If someone threatens immediate harm or you are in danger, contact the appropriate emergency service in your area.
