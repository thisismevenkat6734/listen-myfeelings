Connect safely

A connection cannot be created safely by frontend code alone. The final connection must be authorized by the trusted backend after the rewarded-ad provider verifies the completed ad.

Once authorized, the backend will create the connection with a 12-hour server-controlled expiry. This prevents users from modifying browser storage or timers to bypass the rule.
